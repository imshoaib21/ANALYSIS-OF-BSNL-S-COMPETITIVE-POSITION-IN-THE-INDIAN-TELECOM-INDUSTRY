## Project Overview

India’s telecom industry has changed rapidly with the growth of mobile internet and the adoption of 4G and 5G services. This project explores how BSNL’s competitive position has changed, the challenges behind its declining wireless market share, and the opportunities available through network modernisation, rural connectivity, fibre infrastructure, and better customer service.

The study brings together national and telecom-circle comparisons to develop practical recommendations supported by the report’s analysis.

## Objectives

- Analyse changes in BSNL’s wireless subscriber base and market share.
- Compare BSNL with Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, and Vodafone Idea.
- Examine financial performance and operational improvement.
- Compare BSNL’s position across selected telecom circles.
- Explore the role of delayed technology adoption in competitiveness.
- Identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
- Suggest strategies to improve service quality, customer retention, and regional performance.

## Key Findings

The figures below are reproduced from the submitted project report and refer to its stated study periods.

### Competitive Position — March 2026

| Operator | Wireless subscribers (millions) | Wireless market share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Reliance Jio | 509.21 | 39.71% |
| Bharti Airtel | 481.48 | 37.55% |
| Vodafone Idea | 198.48 | 15.48% |
| BSNL | 92.94 | 7.25% |

### BSNL Subscriber Trend

| Period | Wireless subscribers (millions) |
| --- | ---: |
| March 2022 | 113.74 |
| March 2024 | 88.25 |
| March 2025 | 91.06 |
| March 2026 | 92.94 |

The report identifies a recovery after March 2024, although the subscriber base remained below its March 2022 level. It also highlights that subscriber growth does not necessarily increase market share when competitors grow faster.

### Financial Performance

| Metric | FY2020–21 | FY2024–25 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Total income | ₹18,595 crore | ₹23,427 crore |
| Annual net loss | ₹7,441 crore | ₹2,247 crore |

Income increased and annual losses narrowed over the study period, but BSNL remained loss-making on a full-year basis.

### Regional Performance

Among the selected telecom circles, the report identifies stronger BSNL market shares in Kerala (20.97%), Odisha (14.24%), and Punjab (11.23%), compared with West Bengal (4.51%) and Gujarat (4.55%) in March 2026. These differences support the need for strategies tailored to each circle.

## Strategic Recommendations

- Improve 4G speed, coverage, reliability, and customer experience.
- Develop a phased 5G expansion plan based on demand.
- Use recharge behaviour, complaints, and usage patterns to support customer-retention decisions.
- Tailor network investment and marketing to each telecom circle.
- Strengthen digital customer support and complaint resolution.
- Bundle mobile services with Bharat Fibre, FTTH, and enterprise connectivity.

## Study Limitations

This is a secondary-data research project. Findings reflect the periods and sources documented in the report, rather than live telecom market conditions. Regional analysis covers selected circles, and the study does not include primary customer research or an implemented predictive model.

## Conclusion

The report concludes that BSNL shows signs of stabilisation and financial improvement, while a substantial competitive gap remains. Sustained progress depends on faster technology implementation, better service quality, targeted regional strategies, and effective use of business analytics.
